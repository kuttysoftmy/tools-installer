# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and communicate clearly.
- Focus on what is best for the community and project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or intimidation.
- Publishing private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct and may take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior.
